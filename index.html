The Challenge

Fast fashion and the single-use nature of event wear contribute heavily to environmental waste. Locally, students consistently struggle to find affordable campus uniforms, while expert tailors and rental shops lack the digital visibility to reach them.

Fabric Grid: TexMap exists to bridge this gap. We are building a localized, digital circular economy that keeps textiles in use longer, supporting both students and small businesses in Panabo City.

Our 3 Core Features

Everything you need to navigate the local textile economy.

1. Live GIS Map

A real-time spatial map allowing you to locate textile services. Use interactive filters to sort through tailors, laundries, and rental shops. View pricing and honest community ratings before booking.

2. Event Wear Discovery

Stop buying expensive outfits for a single night. Easily discover, filter, and rent high-quality event wear for school pageants, birthday debuts, or capstone defenses.

3. Circular Marketplace

A P2P listing feed shared by both users and partner shops. Buy, sell, or trade pre-loved campus uniforms and apparel safely.

For Shops: "Boost Listing" to elevate your entire shop's profile and promote services to the top of the map.
For Users: "Boost Product" specifically on your individual products to sell them faster in the feed.
