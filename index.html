The Challenge

Fast fashion and the single-use nature of event wear contribute heavily to environmental waste. Locally, students consistently struggle to find affordable campus uniforms, while expert tailors and rental shops lack the digital visibility to reach them.

Fabric Grid: TexMap exists to bridge this gap. We are building a localized, digital circular economy that keeps textiles in use longer, supporting both students and small businesses in Panabo City.

Core Features

What we offer to the Panabo City community.

Interactive GIS Map

A real-time spatial map allowing users to pinpoint and verify local tailors, laundry services, and repair shops instantly.

Circular Marketplace

A dedicated feed to rent high-value event wear or buy/sell campus uniforms safely with other verified students.

User Dashboard

Manage your active listings, track your favorites, and monitor your personal contribution to reducing textile waste.
