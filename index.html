Why TexMap Exists

The traditional textile lifecycle is broken. We are solving local problems with community-driven solutions.

Textile Waste

Thousands of perfectly good uniforms and garments end up in landfills every year after students graduate or outgrow them.

High Academic Costs

Purchasing brand new uniforms and renting expensive formal wear for single-day events (like capstones) is a financial burden on students.

Scattered Services

Finding reliable, affordable local tailors for repairs or alterations relies on word-of-mouth rather than a verified digital network.

Our Solution

Core Features

Interactive GIS Mapping

Instantly locate verified tailors, laundry services, and rental boutiques in Panabo City. View shop ratings, read community reviews, and directly book services without leaving the app.

Circular Marketplace

A dedicated peer-to-peer exchange for students. Safely buy, sell, or rent campus uniforms and pageant wear. Reduce waste and save money through community commerce.
